Research Profile

Highly motivated Ph.D. candidate with expertise in atmospheric dynamics. My Ph.D. research focuses on the time-mean tropical circulation and the dry dynamics that influence it. I am seeking a postdoctoral fellowship where I can leverage my background in mathematics and physical science to contribute to understanding atmospheric events. Key topics of interest include extreme weather events, wave-mean-flow interactions, and non-linear phenomena.
